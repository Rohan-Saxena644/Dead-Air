## Title

DEAD AIR — A Haunted Archive That Cannot Acknowledge Goodbye | Gemma Gameplay Demo

## Description

An abandoned operations console. A message queued in 2019. A restart that makes another copy, but never brings back a receipt.

DEAD AIR is a playable browser horror investigation built for the Hacktoberfest Open-Source AI Challenge, Week 4: Ghost in the Machine. This is an actual fresh-session gameplay recording at 1920×1080, with captions and no narration or recorded game audio. No dialogue was fabricated and no model waiting time was removed. MORA’s visible mode label identifies the actual inference provider.

Investigate a queue, compare delivery traces, ask MORA to evaluate a theory, repair the incident, and choose the archive’s future. MORA uses Google-hosted Gemma through read-only, discovery-gated tools; a deterministic simulator owns actions, evidence, and consequences.

There are two main endings with four authored outcome variations. Replay changes your decisions; the central mystery stays fixed. The free demo uses temporary saves. Its investigation export contains fictional evidence and metadata, not downloadable audio or proof of a received handover.
